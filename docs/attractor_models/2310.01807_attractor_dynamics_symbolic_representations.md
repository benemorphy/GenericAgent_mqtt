# arXiv:2310.01807 — Discrete, Compositional, and Symbolic Representations through Attractor Dynamics

**Authors:** Andrew Nam, Eric Elmoznino, Nikolay Malkin, James McClelland, Yoshua Bengio, Guillaume Lajoie
**Subjects:** Artificial Intelligence (cs.AI)
**Date:** October 2023 (v1), September 2024

## Abstract
Symbolic systems are powerful frameworks for modeling cognitive processes as they encapsulate the rules and relationships fundamental to many aspects of human reasoning and behavior. Central to these models are systematicity, compositionality, and productivity. However, certain limitations remain: the integration of structured symbolic processes and latent sub-symbolic processes has been implemented through fiat methods such as quantization or softmax sampling, which assume, rather than derive, the operations underpinning discretization and symbolicization. We introduce a novel **neural stochastic dynamical systems model that integrates attractor dynamics with symbolic representations** to model cognitive processes akin to the probabilistic language of thought (PLoT). Our model segments the continuous representational space into discrete basins, with **attractor states corresponding to symbolic sequences**, reflecting semanticity and compositionality through unsupervised learning. Moreover, like PLoT, our model learns to sample a diverse distribution of attractor states that reflect the mutual information between input data and symbolic encodings.

## Key Insights
1. Attractor dynamics → symbolic representations (unsupervised)
2. Continuous space → discrete basins → symbolic sequences
3. Probabilistic Language of Thought (PLoT) framework
4. Integrates symbolic + sub-symbolic processing through neural dynamics
5. Authors include Yoshua Bengio
