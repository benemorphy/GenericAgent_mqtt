## 知识模式

### 领域专有 (8个)
- [95%] Design Specification Documentation: Creating detailed handoff documents that include annotations, measurements, and interaction states for developers.
- [90%] Interactive Prototyping: Building high-fidelity prototypes with micro-interactions and transitions to simulate user experience.
- [90%] 设计过程应遵循结构化流程，从需求分析到高保真原型逐步推进，避免跳跃式设计。
- [88%] Design System Integration: Ensuring consistency through reusable components, tokens, and style guides in handoff.
- [85%] Developer Collaboration Workflow: Establishing efficient review and feedback loops between designers and engineers.
- [85%] 原型应追求高保真度和视觉独特性，避免使用通用模板或默认样式，以增强用户识别度。
- [82%] Asset Export and Optimization: Preparing and exporting design assets (icons, images, fonts) with proper formats and sizes for development.
- [75%] 设计过程中应持续验证原型与用户需求的匹配度，通过迭代反馈优化界面细节。

### 高级模式 (2个)
- [80%] 避免在原型中引入与核心功能无关的装饰性元素，保持界面简洁且聚焦于任务完成。
- [70%] 在UI设计交接中，需明确指定设计交付物（如原型文件、标注规范），确保开发团队准确理解。
